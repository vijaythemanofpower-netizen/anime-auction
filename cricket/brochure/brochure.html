How a game goes

From the hammer to the trophy

01

Retain

Keep up to 6 of last season's players at the real IPL costs (₹18 / 14 / 11 / 18 / 14 Cr; uncapped ₹4 Cr). Every slot you skip becomes a Right to Match card.

02

Bid

Live hammer, official base prices and bid steps, computer rivals. Lose a favourite? Call RTM: the buyer gets one final raise, then you can match it. Unsold players can return in the accelerated round.

03
Your XI

Build

Hidden form is revealed. Pick your XI, batting order, captain, keeper, game plan and an Impact Player. Squad rules keep every team balanced.

04

Play

Bat and bowl every ball: choose the shot or delivery, then stop the timing meter in the green. Toss, DRS, bowling changes and mind games are yours too.

05

Season

A full league with points table and net run rate, then IPL-style playoffs: Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the Final.

06

Glory

Lift the trophy, and see who takes the Orange Cap (most runs) and the Purple Cap (most wickets).
